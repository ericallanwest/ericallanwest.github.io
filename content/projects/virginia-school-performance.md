**[Open the map →](https://ericallanwest.github.io/virginia-school-performance/)** · [Code](https://github.com/ericallanwest/virginia-school-performance)

1,811 schools colored by their 2025-26 performance category, shown over census-tract median
household income.

- **Filter** by category, school level, region and division, or search by name.
- **Click a school** for its framework score and components, its federal identification, its
  2024-25 category and the change since, and its attendance zone (solid where the boundary is
  published, dashed where it's approximate).
- **Analysis panel:** performance against neighborhood income and student poverty.
